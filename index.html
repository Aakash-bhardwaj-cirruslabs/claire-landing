What Claire does

One workforce. Every tool. Zero handoffs.

Claire is built to sit inside how your team already works — not to replace it.

  • Connects your tools

    Slack, GitHub, your calendar, your CRM, your files. Claire plugs into the stack you already run and works across all of it — no migration, no rip-and-replace.

  • Coordinated agents

    Not one chatbot — a team. Specialist agents plan, hand off and review each other's work, so tasks get done end to end instead of stalling at the first tricky step.

  • Always-on menu bar

    Claire lives one click away in your menu bar. Kick off work, check progress, approve a result — without switching apps or breaking your flow. On when you are, on when you're not.

How it works

Live in three steps.

  1. 1

    Connect

    Link the tools your team already uses. Claire reads the context it needs and nothing it doesn't.

  2. 2

  3. 3

    Ship

    Review the result from your menu bar and approve. Work lands while you get on with the rest of your day.
