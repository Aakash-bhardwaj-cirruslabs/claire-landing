What Claire does

One workforce. Every tool. Zero handoffs.

Claire is built to sit inside how your team already works — not to replace it.

  • Connects your tools

    Slack, GitHub, your calendar, your CRM, your files. Claire plugs into the stack you already run and works across all of it — no migration, no rip-and-replace.

  • Coordinated agents

    Not one bot answering one question — a team of specialised agents that hand work off to each other, track state, and finish the job end to end while you focus on what matters.

  • Always-on menu bar

    Claire lives in your menu bar. Delegate a task in seconds, get a notification when it's done. No dashboard to open, no context to rebuild — just results.

How it works

Live in three steps.

  1. 01

    Connect

    Authorise the tools you use. Claire reads your context — open PRs, calendar, inbox, tickets — and builds a live model of your work.

  2. 02

    Delegate

    Type what you need — or just describe the outcome. Claire breaks it into tasks, assigns the right agents, and starts working immediately.

  3. 03

    Ship

    Review the result, approve, and it's done. PRs merged, emails sent, reports filed. Claire handles the last mile so you don't have to.
